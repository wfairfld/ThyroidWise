# ThyroidWise

**Endocrinology AI Consultancy** · [thyroidwise.com](https://thyroidwise.com)

This repository holds the website for ThyroidWise. Wesley P. Fairfield, MD, ECNU, is a board-certified endocrinologist with more than 25 years in practice. Through ThyroidWise he offers non-clinical consulting in endocrinology.

## Consulting areas

- **AI model training and evaluation:** writing and grading expert-level endocrine reasoning tasks, building realistic case sets, and reviewing model output for accuracy, safety and guideline alignment
- **Medical content review:** accuracy review of patient education and clinician-facing material
- **Informatics and governance advisory:** EHR and clinical decision support design, research oversight, and medical staff credentialing

ThyroidWise does not provide medical care, second opinions or medical advice, and no physician–patient relationship is formed.

## About the site

- A single self-contained page (`index.html`). Styles, scripts, the logo and every illustration are built into the file.
- The animated thyroid-axis feedback loop and the gland illustrations are original SVG artwork.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts. There are no other external dependencies, trackers or cookies.
- Animation is paused for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion.

## Hosting

The site is served by **GitHub Pages** from the `main` branch root, with the custom domain `thyroidwise.com` (DNS managed at Squarespace).

To update the site, replace `index.html` in this repository and commit. GitHub Pages republishes automatically within a minute or two. Keep the `CNAME` file; it holds the custom domain setting.
