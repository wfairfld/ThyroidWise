The full endocrine system, with depth in thyroid

Clinical expertise across five areas of endocrinology, drawn from a career of subspecialty consultation.

Thyroid

Nodules and neck ultrasound (AACE ECNU certified), hyper- and hypothyroidism, thyroiditis, goiter, thyroid disease in pregnancy, thyroid cancer.

Pituitary

Pituitary tumors, prolactinomas, Cushing's disease, acromegaly, growth hormone deficiency, hypopituitarism, SIAD and AVP deficiency.

Metabolic bone

Osteoporosis, hyperparathyroidism, hyper- and hypocalcemia, vitamin D deficiency, Paget's disease, fibrous dysplasia.

Adrenal

Adrenal insufficiency, adrenal nodules, glucocorticoid dependency, pheochromocytoma, hyperaldosteronism, secondary hypertension.

Reproductive

Hypogonadism, Klinefelter syndrome, congenital adrenal hyperplasia, female hyperandrogenism, primary ovarian insufficiency, polyglandular autoimmune syndromes.
